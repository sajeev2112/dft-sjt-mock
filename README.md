# DFT SJT Mock Paper

An unofficial practice paper for the UK Dental Foundation Training (DFT) Situational Judgement Test.

**Try it:** https://sajeev2112.github.io/dft-sjt-mock/

- 64 original questions in two papers: Paper 1 (standard) and Paper 2 (harder)
- Ranking items you drag into order, and best-three-of-eight items
- Marking copies the live test: near-miss scoring for rankings, 4 marks per correct pick
- Practice mode (feedback after each question) or exam mode (marked at the end), with a timer set to the live test's pace
- Your answers are saved only in your own browser

The questions are original. They're modelled on patterns in the official 2016 and 2021 DFT practice papers and on GDC and dental defence organisation guidance. The answer keys are reasoned judgements, not official answers. This project isn't affiliated with NHS England, COPDEND, HEIW or NIMDTA.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, with no build step.
